## **Background**

1.  The Company is a worker-owned cooperative governed by its Operating Agreement (the “Operating Agreement”). A founding principle of the Company is that labor owns its work: each worker-owner carries the right to use and build on the work they helped create, during membership and after, for the Company and for themselves.
2.  Section 7.3 of the Operating Agreement grants each Member a perpetual license to Company IP. This instrument evidences that grant in standalone form so the Member holds it in their own hands, independent of the Company’s records. The license is granted by the Operating Agreement itself; this instrument confirms it and does not narrow it.

## **1. Definitions**

1.  **“Company IP”** means all intellectual property owned by the Company at any time during the Membership Period, including the Work Pool (as defined in the Operating Agreement) and all copyrights and other rights in it — source code, game designs, art, music, audio, writing, tools, engines, and related creative and technical works — but excluding the items in Section 5.
2.  **“Membership Period”** means the period from the Member’s admission to the termination of their Membership for any reason.
3.  **“Snapshot Date”** means the date the Member’s Membership terminates.
4.  **“Licensed IP”** means: during the Membership Period, all Company IP as it exists from time to time; and after the Snapshot Date, all Company IP that existed at any time on or before the Snapshot Date.

## **2. Grant**

The Company grants the Member a **worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive** license to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, distribute, publicly perform, publicly display, and create derivative works from the Licensed IP, for any purpose — including commercial purposes and the Member’s own independent projects — without further permission, payment, or accounting to the Company.

## **3. Refresh During Membership; Fixing on Departure**

1.  During the Membership Period, this License refreshes continuously: it always covers the Company IP as it then exists, including work created after this License is signed.
2.  On the Snapshot Date, the scope of this License fixes: it covers everything that existed at any time during the Membership Period, and nothing created after.
3.  On the Member’s written request at or after departure, the Secretary of the Company shall issue, within thirty (30) days, a written schedule identifying the Company IP existing as of the Snapshot Date (including repository identifiers and project names), for attachment to this License as Schedule A. Failure to issue or attach Schedule A does not narrow the License.

## **4. Ownership; Derivative Works**

1.  The Company retains sole ownership of the Licensed IP. This License transfers no ownership and creates no co-ownership.
2.  As between the parties, new material the Member creates in a derivative work under this License belongs to the Member, and the Member may exploit that derivative work freely under this License; the underlying Licensed IP within it remains the Company’s, and the Member’s continued use of it within the derivative is covered by this License.

1.  **Trademarks.** This License does not cover the Company’s trademarks, trade names, or logos, except for truthful, non-confusing attribution (for example, “originally developed at Timeless Way Interactive”).
