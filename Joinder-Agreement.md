## **Background**

The Members approved the New Member's admission on \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ under Sections 2.3
and 5.3(1) of the Operating Agreement. Section 2.3 makes a person a Member on satisfying
four conditions: completing (or being relieved of) the Candidacy Period, Supermajority
approval, receiving and acknowledging the Agreement and any then-current worker handbook,
and executing this Joinder. This Joinder is the fourth.

## **1. Joinder**

The New Member joins the Operating Agreement of the Company as of the Admission Date and
agrees to be bound by it, and by the Certificate of Formation, as fully as if an original
signatory. From the Admission Date the New Member is a Member for all purposes of the
Agreement.

## **2. Receipt and Acknowledgment**

The New Member acknowledges having received and read (a) the Operating Agreement, dated
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, with all amendments through the Admission Date, and (b) the current
worker handbook, dated \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, if one exists, and having had the opportunity
to ask questions about both and to consult their own counsel.

## **3. What the New Member Receives**

On admission, and without any buy-in or capital contribution (Section 8.5), the New Member
holds:

1.  **One Seat and one vote** — equal to every other Member's, regardless of hours, role,
    or seniority (Sections 2.2, 5.2, 8.3);
2.  **The perpetual license** to Company IP under Section 7.3, granted by the Agreement
    itself and evidenced by the Member Perpetual IP License (Exhibit C) executed alongside
    this Joinder, including the delivery right in clause 7 of that Section;
3.  **A Member Account**, opened at a zero balance and maintained under Article 8; and
4.  **Autonomy** — the right to direct their own work under Section 5.1, which is the
    Agreement's default rule and not a privilege granted by anyone.

## **4. What the New Member Agrees To**

1.  **Present assignment of work product.** All intellectual property the New Member
    creates within the scope of their work for the Company is owned by the Company, and
    the New Member makes a present assignment of it under Section 7.1 and will execute any
    further document reasonably needed to perfect that ownership;
2.  **No transfer of membership.** Membership and every governance and economic right
    arising from it are non-transferable; any attempted transfer is void (Section 2.4);
3.  **Other people's information.** The narrow confidentiality obligation of Section 7.6 —
    other people's personal data and third-party NDAs, and nothing more. The New Member
    owes the Company no general duty of secrecy, and nothing here limits their right to
    discuss their compensation, their working conditions, or how the Company is run
    (Sections 3.3, 5.7, 7.6); and
4.  **Governance participation.** To take part in the Committee's reserved and corrective
    matters in good faith under Article 5.
