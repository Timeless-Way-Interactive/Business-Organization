## **Background**

The Company is a worker-owned cooperative governed by its Operating Agreement (the “Operating Agreement”), whose mission is to generate more games, sustainably, with genuine depth. The Company engages contributors both for defined work and as prospective long-term partners: this Agreement includes a defined path toward candidacy for membership (Section 7), and nothing in it is intended to create a permanent class of non-owner labor.

## **1. Engagement and Services**

The Company engages the Contributor, and the Contributor agrees, to perform the services described in Exhibit 1 (the “Services”), including any deliverables and timeline stated there (the “Deliverables”). The Contributor determines the manner, means, schedule, and location of performance. The Contributor may consult the Members for direction, but no Member supervises the Contributor.

## **2. Term and Termination**

1.  This Agreement runs from the Start Date until the Services are complete or the Agreement is terminated.
2.  Either party may terminate this Agreement on fourteen (14) days’ written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for the Contributor’s material breach or unlawful conduct.
3.  Compensation earned or computed through the termination date survives and remains payable per Section 3. Sections 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, and 11 survive termination.
4.  If Option A applies, termination of this Agreement removes the Contributor from the Contractor Pool as of the termination date; amounts computed for the Pool before that date remain payable.
5.  If Option B applies, this Agreement terminates automatically if the Sponsoring Member’s membership in the Company terminates, unless another Member assumes sponsorship in writing.

## **4. Independent Contractor Status**

1.  The Contributor is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, member, or agent of the Company, and has no authority to bind the Company.
2.  The Contributor is responsible for their own taxes, including self-employment taxes. The Company will issue any required information returns (for example, Form 1099).
3.  The Contributor is not eligible for employee benefits and is not covered by the Company’s insurance except as required by law.
4.  The Contributor provides their own equipment and tools unless Exhibit 1 states otherwise.

## **5. Intellectual Property**

1.  **Work made for hire; assignment.** All work product the Contributor creates in performing the Services (the “Work Product”) is a “work made for hire” for the Company to the fullest extent permitted by law. To the extent any Work Product is not a work made for hire, the Contributor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company, upon creation, all right, title, and interest in it, including all copyrights, patent rights, trademark rights, and trade secrets, worldwide.
2.  **Moral rights.** To the extent permitted by law, the Contributor waives, and agrees not to assert, any moral rights in the Work Product against the Company or its licensees.
3.  **Further assurances.** The Contributor will execute any documents the Company reasonably requests to perfect or record the Company’s ownership.
4.  **Pre-existing materials.** Tools, libraries, and materials the Contributor created before or outside this engagement and listed in Exhibit 2 remain the Contributor’s. To the extent the Contributor incorporates any of them into Work Product, the Contributor grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use them as embedded in the Work Product and its derivatives.
5.  **Portfolio right.** After a project is publicly released (or with the Company’s earlier written consent), the Contributor may display excerpts of their contribution in their portfolio, reel, and professional materials, with credit to the Company.

## **6. Credit**

The Contributor will be credited by name (or a professional name they designate in writing) in the credits of any released project to which they contributed. Failure to credit due to good-faith error will be corrected in the next practicable update.

## **7. Path to Candidacy and Membership**

If the Contributor has been engaged continuously for three (3) months or more, the Contributor may request to begin the Candidacy Period described in Section 2.3 of the Operating Agreement, and such a request will not be unreasonably denied. Membership in the Company requires no buy-in and carries an equal seat, an equal share, and an equal vote. On admission as a Member, this Agreement terminates and the person’s work is thereafter governed by the Operating Agreement, including its perpetual IP license to Members.

## **8. Confidentiality — Narrow by Design**

1.  **What is confidential.** “Confidential Information” means only: unreleased builds, source code, and assets not yet made public; access credentials and keys; personal data of players, community members, Members, and other contributors; and third parties’ information the Company is obligated to protect.
2.  **What is never confidential.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Contributor from discussing or disclosing their own compensation, their working conditions, the fact and general nature of this engagement, or from any communication protected by law (including Section 7 of the National Labor Relations Act, to the extent applicable). The Company practices candid discourse and does not use confidentiality to silence workers.
3.  **Duration.** Confidentiality obligations end when the information becomes public through no breach, and in any event three (3) years after termination — except for personal data and third-party obligations, which last as long as the underlying duty.

## **9. No Exclusivity**

This engagement is non-exclusive in both directions. The Contributor may work for others, including other studios, and the Company may engage others for similar services. The Company encourages outside work and asks only that the Contributor avoid unauthorized use of Confidential Information or Work Product.

## **10. Dispute Resolution**

The parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute by direct, candid discussion, and may agree to mediation before pursuing other remedies. Nothing in this Agreement waives the Contributor’s non-waivable statutory rights, including the right to pursue harassment or discrimination claims before the appropriate agency.

## **11. General**

1.  This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.
2.  This Agreement, with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement about its subject and supersedes prior discussions. It may be amended only in a writing signed by all parties.
3.  If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
4.  This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including electronically.
5.  Where this Agreement conflicts with the Operating Agreement, the Operating Agreement controls as among the Company and its Members; this Agreement controls as to the Contributor’s compensation and rights stated here.
